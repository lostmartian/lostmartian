Walk the path with unwavering truth,  
In humble age or eager youth.  
Reject the shadows of a cheated game,  
For hollow wins bring silent shame.  

Reach out a hand when others fall,  
Let deep empathy answer a quiet call.  
To feel another's joy and pain  
Is where our softest strength remains.  

Be whole in spirit, fierce and clear,  
Live without guise, without false fear.  
For a life well-built on honest ground  
Is where true completeness is found.
